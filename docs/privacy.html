TabPocket — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Applies to the TabPocket browser extension

中文版在下方

TabPocket is a local-first browser extension. It does not have a server, does not require an account, and does not transmit your data anywhere. Everything the extension records is stored inside your own browser profile on your own device.

1. What the extension stores

Data Where it lives Why
Saved tab sessions (page titles and URLs) chrome.storage.local on your device To restore a collapsed set of tabs later
Bookmarks and tags you create in TabPocket chrome.storage.local on your device To show and search your bookmark list
Browsing time statistics (domain, hour, duration, open count) IndexedDB on your device To render your own usage charts
Settings (language, excluded domains, tracking on/off) chrome.storage.local on your device To remember your preferences

The extension never uploads, syncs, or backs up this data to any remote server. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising SDK, and no remote code. Removing the extension deletes its local data.

2. Why each permission is requested

PermissionReason
tabs Read the title and URL of open tabs so they can be collapsed into a session and restored, and count which page is currently active for the timer.
storage / unlimitedStorage Save sessions, bookmarks, settings and statistics locally. Unlimited storage is used so long-term statistics do not hit the default quota.
alarms Run a once-per-minute heartbeat that settles how long the current page has been in the foreground. This is the only periodic task.
idle Stop the timer when you are away from the keyboard or the screen is locked, so idle time is not counted as browsing.
bookmarks Read your existing browser bookmarks for the "Organize bookmarks" and "Bookmark audit" features, and let you delete a bookmark from the audit list. This is only used when you open those two features.
history Count how many times a bookmarked page was visited within a chosen period (30 days / 90 days / 1 year) for the audit ranking. History is only read, never modified, and never leaves the device.
contextMenus Add the right-click item "Add this page to TabPocket".

3. Data sharing

None. TabPocket does not sell, transfer, or disclose any user data to third parties. No data is used for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or lending purposes.

4. Your control

  • Statistics can be switched off, and can be cleared at any time from the report page.
  • Excluded domains are never recorded.
  • Sessions and bookmarks can be exported to a JSON file you own, and deleted at will.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all of its stored data.

5. Contact

Questions or requests: GitHub Issues.

中文版

数据全部留在本机

TabPocket 是纯本地浏览器扩展：没有服务器、不需要账号、不上传任何数据。扩展记录的一切都保存在你自己电脑上的浏览器配置目录里。

保存了什么

  • 收拢的标签会话（页面标题与网址）→ 本地 chrome.storage.local
  • 你在扩展内创建的收藏与标签 → 本地 chrome.storage.local
  • 浏览时长统计（域名、小时、时长、打开次数）→ 本地 IndexedDB
  • 设置（语言、排除域名、统计开关）→ 本地 chrome.storage.local

无统计分析、无遥测、无广告 SDK、无远程代码。卸载扩展即删除其本地数据。

权限用途

  • tabs：读取标签页标题与网址，用于收拢/恢复会话与判断当前计时页面。
  • storage / unlimitedStorage：本地保存会话、收藏、设置与统计。
  • alarms：每分钟结算一次当前页面的停留时长，这是唯一的定时任务。
  • idle：离开键盘或锁屏时停止计时，避免挂机刷时长。
  • bookmarks：仅在「整理浏览器收藏」「收藏体检」中使用，读取原生书签并允许删除单条。
  • history：仅用于统计收藏页面在指定区间内的访问次数，只读、不改写、不外传。
  • contextMenus：提供右键「收藏此页面到 TabPocket」。

数据共享

没有。不向任何第三方出售、转让或披露用户数据，也不用于广告、画像、信用评估等用途。

你可以控制什么

  • 统计可随时关闭，也可在报表页一键清空。
  • 加入排除名单的域名不会被记录。
  • 会话与收藏可导出为 JSON 备份，也可随时删除。
