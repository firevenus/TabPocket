No account, credentials or extra information is needed. No sign-up, no server, no network request.

EXPECT EMPTY LISTS ON FIRST INSTALL. Sessions, bookmarks and statistics all start empty. That is the normal initial state, not a broken feature; the steps below fill them in two minutes.

Setup
1. Open 5-6 ordinary web pages in the same window and leave them open.
2. Click the toolbar icon. The popup has four tabs: Sessions / Bookmarks / Stats / About.

Sessions
3. On Sessions click "Collapse all tabs". Those tabs close and a session card appears in the list; expand the card to see its pages.
4. Click "Restore" on the card. The pages open again.

Bookmarks
5. On Bookmarks click "Bookmark this page" to save the current page.
6. Click "Organize bookmarks" and confirm. This reads the browser's existing bookmark tree (this is what the bookmarks permission is for), imports it and tags entries by site category.
7. Set the "Sort" control to "Most visited" to see the list ranked by visit count.
8. Click "Bookmark audit" for the full-page report: bookmarks ranked by how often they were opened over the last 30 / 90 / 365 days, by page or by site, with CSV export.

Stats
9. Browse normally for 2-3 minutes, switching tabs a few times, then open Stats and click "Open full report". A heartbeat settles foreground time once per minute; tab switches settle immediately. The timer pauses while the machine is idle or locked.

About
10. Language switch (English / Chinese), backup and export, excluded domains.

Reviewer notes
- History is read only, never modified: addUrl, deleteUrl, deleteRange and deleteAll are never called.
- The idle state is read only to pause the timer. No keystrokes, clicks or page content are read: the build ships no content script and requests no host permissions.
- All data lives in the browser profile and is removed on uninstall.
- No remote code: no eval, no new Function, no external script, no wasm.
